FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do I book an excursion? +

Complete the booking form on this website. Your details will be prepared and sent directly to Ramudus Express through WhatsApp.

How much is the Warmbaths excursion? +

The Warmbaths / Bela-Bela excursion costs R600 per person. A R200 non-refundable deposit is required.

Where are the pickup points? +

The excursion pickup route runs from Maserumule Park to Groblersdal. Contact us to confirm the pickup point closest to you.

Can I book private transport? +

Yes. Contact Ramudus Express with your travel date, group size, pickup location and destination and we will assist you.
